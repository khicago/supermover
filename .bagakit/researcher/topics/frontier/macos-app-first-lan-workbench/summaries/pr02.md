# CCC verification and history UX summary

## What This Is

- source id: `pr02`

## Why It Matters

High-trust backup UX depends on audit records, per-task history, and verification reports.

## Borrow
- Keep transaction/evidence records central and distinguish source changes, destination changes, missing records, and verification failures.

## Avoid
- Do not use a single green root/status when underlying evidence is missing, stale, or scoped to another source-target pair.

## Bagakit Implication
- Comparator and evidence browser should be artifact-schema-first, with stale/missing evidence states and source-target pair scoping.
